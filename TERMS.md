## Description of Service

SprintScribe AI is a Forge app for Atlassian Confluence that:
- Analyzes meeting transcripts to extract suggestions, decisions, and action items
- Creates Jira issues from extracted action items
- Generates meeting summaries
- Integrates with Atlassian Rovo for AI-powered extraction
